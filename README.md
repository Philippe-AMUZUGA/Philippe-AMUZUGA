# Philippe Amuzuga

**Mechanical engineer, PhD**  
Mechanics · Machine Learning · Computational Modelling and Simulation · Artificial Intelligence · Contact · Fatigue

My research focuses on verifying the structural integrity of mechanical systems by developing advanced analysis tools, and on using machine learning to exploit physical test data and numerical simulation results.

I have also authored scientific publications on the application of machine learning, in particular to developing or optimising design rules and sizing formulas for mechanical components and structures.

I hold a PhD in contact mechanics (INSA Lyon, 2016) and have been at CETIM, the French Technical Centre for the Mechanical Industries, since 2017.

## Selected first-author publications

1. **Recursive Multifactor Regression: A machine learning algorithm for pressure vessel nozzle stress estimation through closed-form expressions**  
   P. Amuzuga, J.-L. Iwaniack, M. Bennebach. *Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence*, 2026. [doi.org/10.1016/j.engappai.2026.115565](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.engappai.2026.115565)  
   A regression algorithm that produces closed-form expressions for nozzle stress in pressure vessels.

2. **Size the reinforcement rings of all your openings with 2pDeE. Envelope formula of mechanical strength through a new synthetic approach: Ordinal Correlation Algorithm**  
   P. Amuzuga, J.-L. Iwaniack. *International Journal of Pressure Vessels and Piping*, 2026. [doi.org/10.1016/j.ijpvp.2026.105801](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijpvp.2026.105801)  
   An envelope strength formula for sizing the reinforcement rings of openings, derived with an ordinal correlation algorithm.

3. **Robustness of a Machine Learning Algorithm for Fatigue Life Estimation Under Data Anomalies**  
   P. Amuzuga, M. Bennebach, J.-L. Iwaniack. *Procedia Structural Integrity*, 2025. [doi.org/10.1016/j.prostr.2025.11.007](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.prostr.2025.11.007)  
   How a machine-learning fatigue-life estimator behaves when the input data contain anomalies.

4. **Model reduction for fatigue life estimation of a welded joint driven by machine learning**  
   P. Amuzuga, M. Bennebach, J.-L. Iwaniack. *Heliyon*, 2024. [doi.org/10.1016/j.heliyon.2024.e30171](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.heliyon.2024.e30171)  
   A machine-learning reduced model that estimates the fatigue life of a welded joint.

5. **Open Gear Rolling Contact Fatigue Life Prediction by a Numerical Approach**  
   P. Amuzuga, B. Depale. *Journal of Tribology*, 2022. [doi.org/10.1115/1.4054728](https://doi.org/10.1115/1.4054728)  
   Fatigue tests on open gears showed a 38 % higher surface-pressure load capacity than ISO 6336 predicts; numerical models were built to predict this effect.

6. **Fully Coupled Resolution of Heterogeneous Elastic-Plastic Contact Problem**  
   K. V. Amuzuga, T. Chaise, A. Duval, D. Nélias. *Journal of Tribology*, 2016. [doi.org/10.1115/1.4032072](https://doi.org/10.1115/1.4032072)  
   From the PhD thesis, published under the name K. V. Amuzuga: a framework for contact between elastic heterogeneities and an elastic-plastic matrix, used to rank the parameters that set the location and size of the maximum plastic strain.
